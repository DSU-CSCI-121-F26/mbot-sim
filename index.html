mbot-sim

A 3D simulator for the mBot2, for CSCI-121. This page is the Maven repository that labs download it from. Source and instructions: github.com/CD-CSCI-10447/mbot-sim.

Add this to a lab's pom.xml:

<repositories>
  <repository>
    <id>mbot-sim</id>
    <url>https://cd-csci-10447.github.io/mbot-sim/maven</url>
  </repository>
</repositories>

<dependencies>
  <dependency>
    <groupId>edu.desu.cis</groupId>
    <artifactId>mbot-sim</artifactId>
    <version>0.1.0</version>
  </dependency>
</dependencies>
